# DuePilot launch film

**The late-payment test** is a 25-second product film following one scripted run of the deployed DuePilot demo. It shows the ₹35,000 outstanding invoice, the real Scenario Lab interaction, a 3D comparison of the two lowest balances and the editable client reminder.

| Time | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Photography footage introduces the problem of getting paid after the job. |
| 0:03.5 | Aperture Studio’s ledger and partially paid invoice INV-0001. |
| 0:06.5 | Scenario Lab moves the expected receipt from 27 November to 12 December. |
| 0:11 | The lowest projected cash shifts from ₹17,000 to −₹18,000, with nine days below zero. |
| 0:16 | The equipment rental comes due first. DuePilot provides an editable reminder. |
| 0:21 | DuePilot and the live demo address. |

The dates and figures come from the fictional Aperture Studio workspace. The 3D chart illustrates the minimum under each scenario. The final file is 1920 × 1080 at 30 frames per second, with H.264 video and stereo audio.
