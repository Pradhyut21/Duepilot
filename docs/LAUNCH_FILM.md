# DuePilot launch film

**The late-payment test** is a 2:10 product film following one scripted run of the deployed DuePilot demo. It shows the partially paid invoice, upcoming bills, baseline cash forecast, a 15-day delay, the resulting shortfall and the editable client reminder.

[Download the extended cut](https://github.com/Pradhyut21/Duepilot/releases/download/v1.0.0-hackathon/duepilot-launch-extended.mp4) · [Open the live demo](https://duepilot-studio-ace.vercel.app/)

| Time | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Photography and wedding footage introduce the risk of getting paid late. |
| 0:08 | Aperture Studio opens with ₹18,000 in recorded cash. |
| 0:17 | Invoice INV-0001 has a ₹20,000 advance and ₹35,000 outstanding. |
| 0:27 | Three scheduled bills total ₹48,000. |
| 0:36 | The 30-day forecast shows a ₹17,000 baseline minimum. |
| 0:45 | The hypothetical receipt moves from 27 November to 12 December. |
| 0:53 | A real Scenario Lab interaction recalculates the projection. |
| 1:03 | The forecast chart shows both curves and the first shortfall. |
| 1:13 | A 3D comparison shows the −₹18,000 minimum and nine negative-cash days. |
| 1:25 | The affected dates show when the gap opens and closes. |
| 1:34 | DuePilot opens the editable reminder and copies it to the clipboard. |
| 1:44 | Reset restores the baseline. The original invoice and payment history remain intact. |
| 2:02 | DuePilot, the live demo address and the music credit. |

The dates and figures come from the fictional Aperture Studio workspace. Before and after the scenario, the live demo returned matching invoice, payment and promise records. The clipboard check confirmed that the reminder referenced INV-0001 and the ₹35,000 balance.
