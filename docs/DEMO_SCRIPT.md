# Three-minute judge walkthrough

Open the live application and select **Explore live demo**. Each visit from the landing page creates a private, resettable sample studio. Its records use fictional names and a fixed 21 November 2026 planning date.

| Time | Action and expected result |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Open Studio overview. Recorded cash is ₹18,000, outstanding invoices total ₹53,500, and scheduled bills total ₹48,000. The ₹6,500 overdue receivable is excluded from the projection. |
| 0:25–0:50 | Open INV-0001 for Arjun & Ananya. The ₹55,000 invoice has a ₹20,000 recorded advance and ₹35,000 remaining. The confirmed promise is dated 27 November. |
| 0:50–1:10 | Open Cash forecast. Inspect receipt assumptions and the daily ledger. The baseline minimum is ₹17,000. |
| 1:10–1:45 | Open Scenario Lab, select Arjun & Ananya and click **Delay by 15 days**. The first negative balance is ₹7,000 below zero on 1 December. The minimum is ₹18,000 below zero on 6 December. The invoice arrives on 12 December in this scenario. |
| 1:45–2:25 | Review affected equipment rental, rent and editing costs. Follow the invoice link to edit and copy a rule-based reminder. Action centre also links the actual overdue invoice to its follow-up draft. |
| 2:25–3:00 | Return to Scenario Lab and reset. The baseline returns to ₹17,000. Open INV-0001 to confirm that its payment, balance and promise are unchanged. Settings offers a separate reset of the sample studio itself. |

To use your own data, start a separate account, enter the opening balance, add a client, save and issue a draft, and record a confirmed payment on its invoice. Browser print supports saving the itemized invoice as a PDF.
